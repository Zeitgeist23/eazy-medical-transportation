About EazyMedicalTransportation.com

EazyMedicalTransportation.com is an independent directory designed to help riders, families, care coordinators and healthcare organizations locate non-emergency medical transportation providers. The site does not operate transportation vehicles and is not owned by or affiliated with the providers listed.

What Eazy does

Eazy organizes public provider records into searchable location and provider pages, adds practical NEMT education, and gives providers a way to claim or update directory information.

What Eazy does not do

A listing is not an endorsement, guarantee of service, proof of insurance participation, or confirmation that a provider offers a specific ride type. Riders should verify details directly with the provider or applicable health plan.
