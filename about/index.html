Independent NEMT Directory

Making medical transportation easier to find.

EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps riders, families, caregivers and healthcare professionals locate independent non-emergency medical transportation providers by location, transportation need and type of medical trip.

What Eazy is

Eazy is a searchable directory and connection platform. We organize provider information so people can compare options and contact transportation companies directly.

✓Search providers by state, city and transportation need.
✓View provider details and request transportation information.
✓Access practical guides about NEMT, wheelchair and dialysis transportation.

Why Eazy exists

Finding dependable transportation for a medical appointment can be confusing. Provider information is often scattered across public records, health-plan networks, local websites and individual transportation companies. Eazy brings that information into one easier-to-search destination.

⌕

Search

Independent by design

Eazy is not a transportation carrier and does not own or operate the providers shown in the directory. That independence is central to the site.

What we do

  • Organize publicly available provider information into searchable pages.
  • Help users discover providers by geography and transportation need.
  • Allow providers to claim and update their directory profiles.
  • Publish practical educational information about non-emergency medical transportation.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate transportation vehicles.
  • We do not guarantee provider availability, service quality or insurance participation.
  • A directory listing is not an endorsement or certification.
  • Users should verify pricing, coverage, credentials and ride details directly with the provider or applicable health plan.

Where provider information comes from

Eazy uses public provider records, including CMS/NPPES information, together with provider-supplied updates and other verified business information where available. Because provider information can change, users should confirm important details directly before arranging transportation.
