Hospital Discharge Transportation

What families and care teams should consider when arranging non-emergency transportation after a hospital discharge.

How do I match the vehicle to the patient?

Confirm whether the passenger can walk, needs a wheelchair-accessible vehicle, requires a stretcher-capable service or needs other assistance.

How should discharge timing be handled?

Discharge times can shift. Confirm how the provider handles delays and whether the facility requires a specific pickup location.

What should be ready at the destination?

Make sure someone can receive the passenger if needed and that stairs, ramps, elevators or mobility equipment at the destination are considered.
