How Non-Emergency Medical Transportation Works

A practical explanation of NEMT, who uses it, how trips are arranged, and what riders should confirm before scheduling.

What is NEMT?

Non-emergency medical transportation helps people reach scheduled healthcare when an ambulance is not required. Trips may include physician visits, dialysis, therapy, outpatient procedures and other planned care.

How are rides arranged?

Depending on the rider and payer, transportation may be arranged directly with a provider, through a managed-care plan, transportation broker, facility, caregiver or private-pay booking.

What should I confirm?

Confirm pickup location, appointment time, mobility needs, return-trip procedure, service area, payment arrangements and whether an escort or special assistance is required.
