How to Choose a Non-Emergency Medical Transportation Provider

Questions to ask when comparing NEMT providers for yourself, a family member or a patient.

Service area and trip type

Confirm that the provider serves both the pickup and destination and can handle one-time or recurring trips.

Accessibility

Describe mobility needs in detail and verify the vehicle and assistance level before scheduling.

Payment and scheduling

Ask about rates or benefits, cancellation rules, scheduling lead time, return-trip procedures and how to reach dispatch on the day of travel.
