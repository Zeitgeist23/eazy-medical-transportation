Medicaid and Non-Emergency Medical Transportation

Understand how Medicaid transportation commonly works and what information riders should verify with their plan or transportation provider.

Coverage varies

Medicaid transportation rules and scheduling procedures vary by state and managed-care plan. Riders should confirm eligibility and authorization requirements with the applicable program.

Plan ahead

Recurring treatments and long-distance trips may require advance scheduling. Keep appointment details, member information and mobility needs available when arranging transportation.

Provider verification

A directory listing does not establish Medicaid participation. Confirm network status and authorization directly with the plan, broker or provider.
