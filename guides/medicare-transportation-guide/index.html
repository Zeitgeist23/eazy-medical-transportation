Medicare Transportation: What to Know

A plain-language guide to Medicare transportation, Medicare Advantage transportation benefits and the difference from Medicaid NEMT.

Traditional Medicare

Routine non-emergency rides are not generally a standard Original Medicare transportation benefit. Coverage circumstances differ from emergency or medically necessary ambulance services.

Medicare Advantage

Some Medicare Advantage plans may include transportation benefits. The member should verify limits, eligible destinations and scheduling procedures with the plan.

Do not assume coverage

Before booking, ask who is responsible for payment and whether prior authorization or use of a designated transportation network is required.
