Private-Pay Medical Transportation

When private-pay NEMT may be useful and what to ask before paying for a ride.

When is private-pay transportation used?

Families may choose private-pay transportation when insurance does not cover the trip, when a preferred provider is out of network, or when a faster scheduling option is needed.

What should a quote include?

Ask whether the quote includes mileage, wait time, wheelchair service, assistance, tolls and the return trip.

What cancellation terms matter?

Understand deposits, cancellation windows and charges if an appointment runs longer than expected.
