Recurring Medical Transportation

A guide to organizing repeat rides for dialysis, therapy, rehabilitation and other ongoing care.

Create a master schedule

Provide treatment days, appointment times, pickup address, facility address and any recurring mobility needs.

Use consistent contacts

Keep the same rider, caregiver, facility and dispatch contact information whenever possible.

Review changes

Reconfirm holidays, treatment schedule changes and transportation authorizations before they become missed trips.
