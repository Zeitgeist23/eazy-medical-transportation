Medical transportation in Chicago

Non-emergency medical transportation can help Chicago-area patients reach hospitals, physician offices, dialysis centers, rehabilitation facilities and other healthcare destinations when ordinary transportation is not practical.

Types of rides

Depending on the provider, available services may include ambulatory transportation, wheelchair-accessible rides, dialysis transportation, recurring appointment transportation and other scheduled medical trips.

Choosing a Chicago NEMT provider

Confirm that the provider serves both the pickup location and destination, can accommodate the rider's mobility needs, has availability for the requested appointment time and accepts the intended payment method or health-plan arrangement.

Chicago-area service coverage

Providers may serve the City of Chicago, nearby suburbs, Cook County and surrounding counties. Service boundaries differ by company, so users should verify coverage directly before scheduling.

Use Eazy to locate providers

Eazy Medical Transportation is an independent directory. It does not represent that every listed provider serves every Chicago neighborhood or accepts every insurance plan. Provider details should be confirmed directly before booking.
