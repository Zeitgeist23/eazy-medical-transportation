Non-Emergency Medical Transportation in Cook County, Illinois

People throughout Cook County may need scheduled transportation to hospitals, clinics, dialysis facilities, rehabilitation centers and physician offices. EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps riders and families locate independent NEMT providers and contact them directly about transportation needs.

Finding the right provider

Transportation requirements differ by passenger. Some riders are ambulatory, while others may need a wheelchair-accessible vehicle or assistance getting to and from the vehicle. When comparing providers, describe the passenger's needs and both the pickup and healthcare destination.

Common NEMT trips

  • Doctor and specialist appointments
  • Dialysis and recurring treatment
  • Physical therapy and rehabilitation
  • Outpatient procedures
  • Wheelchair-accessible healthcare transportation

Independent provider directory

Eazy does not provide transportation services and does not guarantee availability. Verify service areas, accessibility, credentials, pricing and payment arrangements directly with the provider.
