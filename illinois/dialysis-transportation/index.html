Dialysis Transportation in Illinois

Patients receiving dialysis often need dependable transportation several times each week. EazyMedicalTransportation.com is an independent directory designed to help Illinois riders, families and care coordinators locate non-emergency medical transportation providers and ask about recurring dialysis trips.

Planning recurring dialysis rides

When contacting a transportation provider, give the dialysis facility address, treatment schedule, expected pickup window and any mobility assistance requirements. Because treatment end times can vary, riders should also ask how return transportation is coordinated.

What to confirm before scheduling

  • Whether the provider serves both the pickup address and dialysis facility.
  • Whether recurring trips can be scheduled in advance.
  • Whether wheelchair-accessible service or other mobility assistance is available when required.
  • How return pickups are handled when treatment times change.
  • What payment arrangements or transportation benefits the provider accepts.
