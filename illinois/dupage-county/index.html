Non-Emergency Medical Transportation in DuPage County, Illinois

EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps riders and families locate independent non-emergency medical transportation providers serving DuPage County. Trips may include scheduled medical appointments, therapy, dialysis, outpatient care and other healthcare destinations where emergency ambulance service is not required.

Planning a DuPage County medical ride

When contacting a provider, share the pickup address, destination, appointment time and any mobility assistance required. Ask whether the provider serves your community and whether recurring trips can be scheduled in advance.

Services to ask about

  • Ambulatory medical transportation
  • Wheelchair-accessible vans
  • Dialysis and recurring treatment rides
  • Rehabilitation and therapy appointments
  • Hospital discharge and outpatient procedure transportation

Compare independent providers

Provider service areas, accessibility, pricing and payment arrangements vary. Eazy is a directory and does not operate the listed transportation companies, so verify all trip details directly with the provider.
