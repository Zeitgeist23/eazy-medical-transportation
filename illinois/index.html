Finding NEMT in Illinois

Illinois riders may use non-emergency medical transportation for scheduled healthcare trips such as physician visits, dialysis, rehabilitation, outpatient care and other routine appointments. Available services vary by city, county, provider and payer.

Common transportation needs

Ambulatory rides

Transportation for riders who can enter and exit a vehicle with limited or no assistance.

Wheelchair rides

Accessible transportation for riders who travel using a wheelchair.

Dialysis trips

Recurring rides to dialysis centers and return transportation after treatment.

Medical appointments

Trips to hospitals, clinics, specialists, rehabilitation and outpatient facilities.

Payment and eligibility

Some transportation may be privately paid while other rides may be covered through Medicaid, managed-care plans or other programs. Coverage and prior authorization rules can differ, so riders should confirm eligibility and scheduling requirements with their health plan or transportation provider.
