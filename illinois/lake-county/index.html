Non-Emergency Medical Transportation in Lake County, Illinois

EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps people search for independent NEMT providers serving Lake County and surrounding communities. Non-emergency medical transportation can help eligible or private-pay passengers reach scheduled healthcare appointments when an ambulance is not required.

Transportation needs in Lake County

Riders may need transportation to hospitals, physician offices, rehabilitation centers, dialysis facilities, outpatient clinics and other healthcare destinations within Lake County or elsewhere in the Chicago metropolitan area. Providers differ in service area, vehicle type and mobility assistance.

Services to ask providers about

  • Ambulatory medical transportation
  • Wheelchair-accessible transportation
  • Recurring dialysis transportation
  • Rehabilitation and therapy appointments
  • Hospital discharge and outpatient procedure transportation

Before arranging a trip

Confirm the provider's service area, pickup time, accessibility capabilities, pricing or benefit eligibility, and return-trip procedure. Eazy is an independent directory and does not operate the transportation companies listed.
