Non-Emergency Medical Transportation in McHenry County, Illinois

EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps riders, families and care coordinators find independent providers serving McHenry County for scheduled healthcare transportation.

Trips may include

Doctor visits, dialysis, rehabilitation, outpatient treatment, hospital discharge and other non-emergency medical appointments. Ask providers whether they serve your pickup location and destination before scheduling.

What to confirm

  • Service area and travel distance
  • Wheelchair accessibility when needed
  • Recurring appointment scheduling
  • Pickup and return-trip procedures
  • Pricing, benefits and accepted payment arrangements

Eazy is an independent directory and does not provide transportation services.
