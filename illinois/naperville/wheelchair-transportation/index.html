Wheelchair Transportation in Naperville, Illinois

EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps riders, families and care coordinators locate independent providers that may offer wheelchair-accessible non-emergency medical transportation in Naperville.

Wheelchair-accessible healthcare trips

Accessible transportation may be needed for physician appointments, dialysis, therapy, rehabilitation, outpatient procedures and other scheduled medical visits.

Verify the service before booking

  • Vehicle accessibility and wheelchair compatibility
  • Ramp or lift availability
  • Mobility and boarding assistance
  • Service-area coverage
  • Recurring trip and return-pickup options
