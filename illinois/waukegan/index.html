Non-Emergency Medical Transportation in Waukegan, Illinois

EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps riders and families find independent NEMT providers serving Waukegan and surrounding Lake County communities.

Medical transportation needs

Trips may include physician visits, dialysis, rehabilitation, outpatient procedures, hospital discharge and other scheduled healthcare transportation. Provider service areas and vehicle capabilities vary.

What to confirm

  • Pickup and destination coverage
  • Wheelchair-accessible vehicle availability
  • Recurring scheduling for treatments
  • Return-trip procedures
  • Pricing and accepted payment arrangements
