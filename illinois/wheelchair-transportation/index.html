Wheelchair Transportation in Illinois

EazyMedicalTransportation.com helps riders, families and care coordinators find independent non-emergency medical transportation providers in Illinois. Wheelchair-accessible transportation may be useful for passengers who cannot safely transfer into a standard passenger vehicle or who need a wheelchair-accessible van for a healthcare trip.

Common wheelchair transportation trips

Wheelchair NEMT may be used for physician appointments, dialysis, rehabilitation, outpatient procedures, hospital discharge, therapy visits and other non-emergency healthcare transportation. Service capabilities vary by provider, so riders should confirm accessibility and assistance requirements directly before arranging a trip.

Questions to ask a provider

  • Does the vehicle accommodate the passenger's wheelchair type and size?
  • Is assistance available from the pickup location to the vehicle?
  • Does the provider serve the required pickup and destination areas?
  • Can the provider accommodate recurring appointments?
  • What payment methods, insurance programs or transportation benefits are accepted?

Finding Illinois providers

Use the Eazy directory to compare independent providers and contact them about service areas, vehicle accessibility and trip requirements. A directory listing does not guarantee that a provider offers wheelchair transportation; always verify the service directly.
