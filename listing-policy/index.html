Provider Listing Policy

Provider profiles may originate from public CMS NPPES records. Listing does not imply a commercial relationship with EazyMedicalTransportation.com.

Claims and corrections

Providers may use the Claim / Update This Profile process to submit corrections and richer service information. Submitted information should accurately describe the provider and may be reviewed before publication.

Service claims

Eazy avoids assigning service-specific claims such as wheelchair, dialysis or stretcher transportation unless there is explicit provider-supplied or otherwise reliable evidence supporting the classification.
