Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)

Non-emergency medical transportation helps people reach healthcare appointments when they need transportation assistance but do not require emergency ambulance care.

Find Transportation Providers

What is non-emergency medical transportation?

NEMT is transportation for scheduled or routine healthcare trips. Riders may need help because they do not drive, cannot safely use ordinary transportation, use a wheelchair, need assistance getting to recurring treatment, or have another mobility limitation.

Common NEMT trips

Doctor appointments

Transportation to primary care, specialists, follow-up visits and outpatient clinics.

Dialysis

Recurring transportation to and from dialysis treatment centers.

Rehabilitation

Trips to physical therapy, occupational therapy and rehabilitation appointments.

Wheelchair transportation

Transportation using appropriately equipped vehicles for riders who use wheelchairs.

How Eazy Medical Transportation works

Eazy Medical Transportation is an independent directory designed to help riders, families and care coordinators locate non-emergency medical transportation providers. Availability, service areas, eligibility and payment arrangements vary by provider.

Before booking a ride

Confirm the pickup address, appointment time, return-trip needs, mobility requirements and whether the provider accepts the rider's payment method or health plan. If a rider is experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services rather than using NEMT.
