What is wheelchair medical transportation?

Wheelchair transportation is a form of non-emergency medical transportation for riders who use wheelchairs and need an accessible vehicle. Depending on the provider, vehicles may include ramps or lifts and securement systems designed for wheelchair riders.

When wheelchair transportation may be useful

Common trips include doctor visits, dialysis, rehabilitation, outpatient procedures, diagnostic testing, hospital discharge transportation and recurring treatment appointments.

What to confirm with a provider

Before scheduling, ask whether the provider can accommodate the rider's wheelchair type and dimensions, whether assistance is available from the pickup point, whether stairs are involved, how return transportation is handled and what forms of payment or insurance are accepted.
